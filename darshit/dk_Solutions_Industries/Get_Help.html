Priority Support

Benefits

With your active Priority Support, you can get assistance through the Online Service Center for your Intel® toolkits.

  • Submit questions, problems, and other technical support issues through direct and private interactions with Intel's engineers.
  • Monitor previously submitted issues.
  • Get accelerated response time for technical questions and other product needs. 
  • Get Priority Support for escalated defects and feature requests.
