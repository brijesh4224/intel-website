SOFTWARE SAMPLE

AI Connect for Scientific Data

AI Connect for Scientific Data (AiCSD) is an open source software sample that connects data from scientific instruments to applicable AI pipelines and runs workloads at the network edge.

With this software sample, you can automate AI capabilities for scientific data to deliver faster insights, connect machine learning models and pipelines from their training environment to the edge, and integrate AI pipelines into edge computing solutions.
